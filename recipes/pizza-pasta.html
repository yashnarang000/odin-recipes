Pizza Pasta

Description

Pizza pasta is a delicious fusion dish that combines classic pizza ingredients with a pasta base. It typically features cooked pasta (like rotini, penne, or macaroni) tossed with rich, herb-infused pizza or marinara sauce. It is then mixed with traditional pizza toppings—like pepperoni, sausage, vegetables, and plenty of melted mozzarella cheese.

Ingredients

  • 8 ounces rotini pasta
  • 1 pound lean ground beef
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 1 (28 ounce) jar spaghetti sauce
  • 4 ounces sliced pepperoni sausage
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C).
  2. Bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a boil. Add pasta and cook for 8 to 10 minutes or until al dente; drain.
  3. In a medium skillet over medium-high heat, cook beef with onion until beef is brown. Drain. Combine beef mixture with spaghetti sauce, pepperoni and cooked pasta and pour into a 9x13 inch baking dish. Top with mozzarella.
  4. Bake in preheated oven for 30 minutes, until cheese is melted and golden.
